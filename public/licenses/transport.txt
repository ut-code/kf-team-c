航空路データ: OpenFlights / Airline Route Mapper / OurAirports
https://openflights.org/data.php
https://github.com/jpatokal/openflights/tree/master/data

https://github.com/eurostat/searoute

EUPL 1.2: searoute-EUPL.txt を同梱。ゲーム用に12経路を抽出。
